# About Talaria Guild

We're on a mission to make professional AI automation accessible to small service businesses.

## Our Story

Founded in 2026, Talaria Guild was born from a simple observation: small businesses were being left behind in the AI revolution. Enterprise software was too expensive and complicated. DIY solutions required technical expertise most business owners didn't have.

We set out to change that.

## What We Do

We build custom AI automation systems for cleaning services and property management companies. Our systems handle:

- Customer intake and scheduling
- Job dispatching and tracking
- Follow-ups and reminders
- Reporting and analytics

All running 24/7, never missing a booking, and saving our clients 15-20 hours per month.

## Our Approach

**White-Glove Service**: We handle everything from setup to ongoing optimization. You focus on your business, we handle the automation.

**Custom-Built**: Every business is different. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all SaaS. Your system is tailored to your specific workflow.

**Transparent Pricing**: $1,500 setup + $350-500/month. No hidden fees, no per-seat charges, no surprises.

**Results-Driven**: We measure success by hours saved and revenue generated, not features shipped.
