# Property Management Automation

Save 15-20 hours per month on maintenance coordination and tenant communication.

## What We Automate

### Maintenance Request Intake
- Tenants text, email, or message on WhatsApp
- AI extracts: unit number, issue type, urgency, tenant contact
- Creates job card automatically
- Confirms receipt with tenant

### Intelligent Triage
- Emergency (pipe burst, no heat): Immediate notification to manager
- Urgent (broken appliance, leak): Next business day
- Routine (cosmetic, non-critical): Scheduled batch

### Job Tracking
- All requests visible on Planka board
- Move cards through workflow: Pending → Assigned → In Progress → Complete
- Automatic status updates to tenants
- Contractor communication coordination

### Reporting
- Monthly maintenance summaries
- Response time tracking
- Cost analysis by unit/building
- Trend identification
