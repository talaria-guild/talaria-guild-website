# Cleaning Services Automation

Never miss a booking again. Save 15-20 hours per month on scheduling and customer communication.

### Customer Intake
- Customers text/email/message for quotes

- Provides instant quote
- Books appointment automatically

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- AI coordinates with your availability

- Reduces no-shows by 40%+
